Lux Invicta Restored Edition is built on CleanSlate (1.0 beta 7) and uses content from CK2Plus
(the CK2Plus AIO version), both by the CK2Plus team, used with thanks:

- CleanSlate: the fixed and reorganised base game scripts this edition is rebuilt on
- CK2Plus: Holy Fury content, monastic orders, plots, the Roman provincia restoration and many
  updated events and decisions

Lux Invicta also carries content from these mods, used with their creators' permission and with thanks:

- VIET (VIET Events): flavour events, artifacts, deaths, ambitions, nicknames and game rules
- SELIN: the realm law paths (clerical, martial, messianic, populist, scholarly, statist,
  traditional and tribal) with their modifiers, traits, decisions and events
